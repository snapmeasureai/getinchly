The short version

  • Inchly has no account. Your doses, weights, side effects, check-ins and body scans are stored on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The only part of your log that ever leaves your phone is a body scan you choose to make: two photos and your height go to our scanning service, which processes them in memory, sends back your 3D body and measurements, and never saves the photos.
  • There is no advertising or analytics code in the app. We don’t sell or share your information.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Delete all data.

1. Who we are and what this covers

Inchly is made by SnapMeasureAI, LLC (“we”, “us”), an Illinois limited liability company at 119 South Western Avenue, Unit 1 #488, Chicago, IL 60612, USA. This policy covers the Inchly app for iPhone and Android (the “App”) and this website, getinchly.com (the “Website”).

2. What stays on your phone

The App has no account and no cloud copy. Everything you enter is stored in the App’s own storage on your phone:

  • Your profile: an optional first name, units, height, starting and goal weight, your medication, dose and dose day, reminder settings, and protein and water goals.
  • Your dose log: date and time, medication, dose, injection site, any note, and the doses you have on hand if you track supply.
  • Weigh-ins.
  • Daily check-ins: side effects, food noise, protein and water.
  • Body scans: measurements, the 3D body model, the date and any note.
  • Progress photos, only if you turn on Keep progress photos: the two photos from each body scan, so you can see them side by side. This is off until you turn it on.

We can’t see, recover or restore any of this. You can delete single entries, or everything at once in Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the App also removes it.

Backups and exports. Because there is no account, the App can save a backup file of everything it keeps, including any progress photos, so you can move to a new phone. It can also make a PDF report, a CSV file or a share card. All of these are made on your phone and go only where you choose to save or send them. We never receive them. Anyone who has a backup file or report can read it, so keep it somewhere private.

Your phone’s own backups (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android) may include App data if you have them turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple or Google, not by us.

3. Body scans

When you make a body scan, and only then, the App sends us:

  • a front photo and a side photo, shrunk to about 800 pixels on your phone first; and
  • your height.

You agree to this each time by ticking the box before the scan. Your weight, your medication and everything else in your log are never sent.

The photos and height travel encrypted (TLS) to our scanning servers, which Amazon Web Services runs for us in the United States. The servers process them in memory and send back your measurements and a 3D body model. Then:

  • Photos are never saved. They exist only in memory while your scan is made. They are never saved to files, kept in logs, or seen by people. (You can choose to keep your own copy on your phone; see section 2.)
  • Measurements and the 3D model are not kept by us. They go back to your phone, and our copy is discarded.
  • Your photos are never used to train or improve our models.
  • No one is identified. The software locates body landmarks to estimate shape and size. It does not recognize people and does not create or keep face templates, faceprints or any other biometric identifier.

The scanning servers keep a short technical log of each request, such as the time, the result (for example “scan completed” or “no person found”) and how long it took, so we can run the service and fix problems. The log never contains photos, heights, measurements or IP addresses, and it is deleted after a few days. We also keep running totals of scans made by the App, such as how many scans it made in a minute and how many found no person. These totals identify no one and are kept for up to 15 months for capacity planning.

To stop abuse, the servers limit how many scans can come from one network. For this they keep short-lived counters filed under a one-way, keyed scramble (hash) of your IP address, never the address itself, and the counters are gone within a day.

Only scan yourself, or an adult who has agreed to be scanned.

4. Reminders

Dose reminders, the monthly body scan reminder, the reorder reminder and the reminder before a free trial ends are scheduled by the App on your phone, as local notifications. We don’t run a notification server and have no way to contact your phone. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your phone’s settings.

5. Apple Health

On iPhone, if you turn on sync, the App writes your weigh-ins and the waist size from each body scan to Apple Health, and reads your latest weight to fill in a weigh-in. If you also turn on the import (part of Inchly Plus), the App reads your weight readings and your daily totals of calories, protein, fiber and water from Apple Health, such as those a food-tracking app saved there, and shows them in the App. This all happens on your phone. We never receive data from Apple Health. It is not used for advertising or marketing, not sold, and not shared. You can turn sync and the import off in the App, and manage or delete that data, and what the App may read, in the Health app. The Android version of the App doesn’t connect to Health Connect yet.

6. Subscriptions

Inchly Plus is bought and billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles your payment details, and we never receive them.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to confirm which subscription is active on your phone. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID that is not linked to your name or email, your purchase and subscription history from the store with transaction IDs, the store country and price, and basic device information such as the operating system and app version. It receives nothing from your log. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

7. This website and email

The Website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it loads nothing from other sites. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email us, we receive what you send, such as your email address and message. We use it only to reply and keep it for as long as needed to help you, plus a reasonable period for our records. Don’t send us health details you don’t need to.

8. What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t share it for targeted or cross-context advertising.
  • We don’t receive or keep your log, your photos, your measurements or your 3D models.
  • We don’t use your photos to train our models.
  • We don’t identify people from photos or create biometric identifiers.
  • We don’t put advertising or analytics code in the App.

9. Who we share information with

We share only what each provider needs to do its job for us:

  • Amazon Web Services: runs the servers that process body scans.
  • Apple and Google: distribute the App and handle subscription payments.
  • RevenueCat: checks subscription status (see section 6).
  • GitHub: hosts the Website.
  • jsDelivr: delivers the open-source code the App uses to draw 3D bodies.
  • Our email provider: delivers messages you send us.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people’s safety or our rights, or to a buyer or successor if our business is sold or reorganized. In a sale or reorganization, this policy would continue to apply.

11. Security

Your log is kept in the App’s private storage on your phone, protected by your phone’s lock and encryption. Body scans travel over TLS 1.2 or higher. Photos are processed in memory on servers whose file systems are read-only, with no swap space and crash dumps turned off, so photos can’t be written to disk. Administration requires multi-factor authentication and is limited to the people who run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but keeping as little as possible is our main protection: we can’t lose what we don’t have.

12. Your choices and rights

Almost all of your information is already in your hands. You can change or delete it in the App at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask whether we hold personal information about you, get a copy of it, have it corrected or deleted, object to or restrict how we use it, withdraw consent, and not be treated differently for using these rights. Email info@snapmeasureai.com with your request. We may need to verify that the request comes from you. We answer within 30 days, or within the time your local law sets, which can be up to 45 days. Because we don’t keep your log, photos or scans, we will usually have no such data to return or delete. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and you can complain to your local data protection or consumer protection authority.

13. Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Some US state laws, including Washington’s My Health My Data Act and similar laws in Nevada and Connecticut, give extra protection to consumer health data. In Inchly that includes your medication and doses, side effects, weight and body measurements.

  • What we collect: only the two photos and height sent for a body scan you ask for, and the measurements and 3D model made from them. Everything else in your log stays on your phone and is never collected by us.
  • Why: only to make the body scan you asked for.
  • Where it comes from: you, through the App.
  • Who it is shared with: only Amazon Web Services, which runs the scanning servers for us. We don’t sell it.
  • How long: only while the scan is processed, usually a few seconds.
  • Your rights: you can ask whether we hold your consumer health data, ask for a copy, ask us to delete it, and withdraw consent, as described in section 12. You can appeal a decision by replying to our answer, and if we deny the appeal you can contact your state’s Attorney General.

15. Children
