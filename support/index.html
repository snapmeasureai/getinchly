Getting started

Is Inchly free?

Yes. The dose log, reminders, site rotation, supply count, weight, side effects and daily check-ins, one body scan a month with your first and latest side by side in 3D, milestones, the share card, backup and Apple Health (on iPhone) are free, with no time limit. Inchly Plus is optional. It adds your estimated medication level, unlimited body scans, any two scans side by side in 3D with a map of where you changed, your journey in 3D, progress photos side by side, how each dose step went, your goal date and a doctor report.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, email or password. Everything you log is kept on your phone.

Which medications does Inchly support?

Zepbound, Mounjaro, Wegovy (including Wegovy HD), Ozempic, Foundayo, the Wegovy pill, Rybelsus, Saxenda, and compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide. Taking something else? Choose Other and type its name. You can change your medication any time in Settings → Medication.

Inchly Plus

How does the free trial work?

When a plan offers a free trial, Plus unlocks straight away. Inchly reminds you two days before the trial ends. If you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, you aren’t charged.

How do I cancel Plus?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not in the app:

  • iPhone: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Inchly → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Inchly → Cancel.

In Inchly, Settings → Manage subscription takes you there. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I have Plus but the app doesn’t show it

Go to Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store and Google Play purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com or in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. We don’t have a copy, so we can’t see it, and we can’t restore it if your phone is lost. Save a backup now and then (see below).

How do I move to a new phone?
  1. On your old phone: Settings → Backup → Back up now, and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Files.
  2. On the new phone: install Inchly, then Settings → Backup → Restore from a backup, and choose the file.

Restoring replaces what is on the new phone. Your subscription comes back with Restore purchases.

How do I delete my data?

Delete single entries in History (Today → See all entries), or everything at once in Settings → Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also deletes it. Your subscription is not affected; cancel it with Apple or Google.

What happens to my body scan photos?

The two photos and your height are sent, encrypted, to our scanning service. It processes them in memory to make your 3D body and measurements, and never saves them. They are never used for training. If you turn on Keep progress photos, a copy stays on your phone only. See the privacy policy.

Reminders

My reminders don’t arrive
  • Check that notifications are allowed for Inchly in your phone’s settings.
  • In Inchly, check Settings → Reminders and the time of day you chose.
  • On Android, battery saving can delay reminders. Set Inchly’s battery use to Unrestricted in your phone’s app settings.

The dose reminder comes on the day a dose is due and moves each time you log one. If you log a dose before the reminder, it won’t nag you.

Body scans

Tips for a good scan
  • Wear fitted clothes, such as leggings or shorts and a T-shirt, and keep hair off your neck.
  • Prop the phone up at about waist height and step back until your whole body is in the frame, head to feet.
  • Stand on a plain floor with good light. Arms relaxed, a little away from your body.
  • Use the same clothes, spot and time of day each time for the cleanest comparison.
The scan says it couldn’t find a person

Make sure your whole body, from the top of your head to your feet, is inside the frame in both photos, with nothing in front of you, and try again.

Why do the numbers differ from my tape measure?

The scan estimates your measurements from photos, and a tape depends on exactly where and how tightly it’s held. Compare scans with scans, taken the same way, to see your trend.

Medical questions

Can Inchly tell me what dose to take?

No. Inchly is a personal log, not medical advice. The medication level is an estimate from your logged doses, not a blood test. Always follow your prescriber’s instructions, and contact a health professional about side effects or missed doses.
