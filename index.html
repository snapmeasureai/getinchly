GLP-1 tracker questions

Is Inchly free?

Yes. Logging your doses, weight and side effects, reminders and one body scan a month are free, with no time limit. Inchly Plus is optional and adds the medication level, unlimited scans, your 3D journey and reports.

Which GLP-1 medications can I track?

Any weekly or daily GLP-1 shot or pill: semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus), tirzepatide (Zepbound, Mounjaro), liraglutide (Saxenda), orforglipron (Foundayo), compounded semaglutide and tirzepatide, or a medication you add yourself.

Can Inchly track inches lost, not just weight?

Yes. On a GLP-1 the scale can stall while your waist keeps shrinking. The body scan measures your waist, hips, chest, thighs and arms from two photos taken at home, so you can see the inches you lose over time.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Everything you log is kept on your phone. To move to a new phone, save a backup file in Settings and restore it on the new phone.

How does the body scan work?

Prop your phone up, step back and follow the voice timer: one photo from the front, one from the side. The photos and your height are sent, encrypted, to our scanning service, which makes your 3D body and measurements and deletes the photos.

Is Inchly on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Inchly is a free app for iPhone and Android phones.

Is Inchly medical advice?

No. Inchly is a personal log to help you see your patterns. It doesn’t tell you when or how much to take. Always follow your prescriber’s instructions.
