3. Inchly is not medical advice

Inchly is a personal log for general wellness and your own records. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition. It does not tell you whether, when or how much medication to take. Always follow your prescriber’s instructions and the information that comes with your medication.

  • Medication level. The estimated level is calculated from the doses you log and the medication’s published half-life. It is an estimate for spotting patterns, not a blood level, and real levels differ from person to person.

4. Body scans are estimates

The body scan estimates measurements and a 3D body model from two photos. Results depend on lighting, camera angle and distance, your pose, your clothing (fitted clothes work best) and the height you enter. They can differ from scan to scan and from tape-measure results. Don’t rely on them for medical decisions.
